AI Grand Prix – Virtual Qualifier 1 Simulator Access Guide

Hi,

The wait is over — **Virtual Qualifier 1 is now live.**  
To help you get started as quickly as possible, we have prepared a step-by-step guide below for downloading, installing, and accessing the Simulator.

**Step 1: Access Your Dashboard**  
Log in to the AI Grand Prix Team Portal using your team account and navigate to your Dashboard.  
On your Dashboard, locate Virtual Qualifier 1 and click Download Simulator.

**Step 2: Create Your Simulator Account**  
Before downloading the simulator, you will be asked to create your simulator account:

• Set a password  
• Confirm your password  
• Click Create Simulator Account

The email address for your Simulator Account is the same like your team account and you will be allocated to your team automatically, but please note that the simulator account is separate from your AI Grand Prix Team Portal account.

**Step 3: Verify Your Email Address**  
After creating your simulator account, verify your email address using the verification email you receive.  
Once verified, you can continue with the simulator download or return to your Dashboard.

**Step 4: Download and Extract the Files**  
Choose a location on your computer where you would like to save the download package.

After extracting the initial ZIP file, you will find:  
• AIGP\_Simulator.zip  
• PyAIPilotExample.zip  
• README.md

Please extract both:  
• AIGP\_Simulator.zip  
• PyAIPilotExample.zip

**Step 5: Start the Simulator**  
Open the extracted AIGP\_Simulator folder.  
Launch the simulator by double-clicking → FlightSim.exe

**Step 6: Review the Python Example Files (Optional)**  
The PyAIPilotExample folder contains example Python files that can help you get started:

• controller.py  
• main.py  
• mavlink\_rx.py  
• requirements.txt  
• setup.py  
• timesync.py  
• vision\_rx.py

You may use these files within your preferred Python development environment.

**Step 7: Log In to the Simulator**  
When the simulator starts, log in using your simulator account credentials.  
Use the email address you verified before and password you created during simulator account setup and click Submit.

**Step 8: Join Virtual Qualifier 1**  
Inside the simulator:  
• Open Active Events  
• Select AI-GP Virtual Qualifier #1

The simulator will then load the competition map.  
You are now ready to begin developing, testing, and competing in Virtual Qualifier 1.

**Need Help?**  
If you encounter any issues during installation or setup, please contact the AI Grand Prix Team and we’ll be happy to assist.

We are excited to finally see your solutions take flight and wish all teams the best of luck in Virtual Qualifier 1.

Good luck and happy coding,  
The AI Grand Prix Team
